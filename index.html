ZONE 3 OF 7 • NEURAL CORE

The Brain of the City

Neural Core with circuit boards, processing units, and robotic maintenance crew

Deep beneath the city lies the Neural Core - the brain of Gen AI Robot City. It isn't made of magic: it is made of algorithms and mathematical equations, running on the circuit boards you see.

🧮 What's inside the Neural Core?

📜 Algorithms are step-by-step instructions - just like a recipe. For example: "1. Read the visitor's name. 2. Save it on the visitor board. 3. Say hello!"
➗ Mathematical equations help the AI make decisions. Each tiny "neuron" multiplies numbers by their importance (called weights), adds them up, and decides whether to "fire".
output = activation( w₁×x₁ + w₂×x₂ + b )

Try it - should a robot go to the Robo Café?
Battery is low (x₁ = 1) × importance (w₁ = 3) + café is nearby (x₂ = 1) × importance (w₂ = 1) + bias (b = −2)
= 3 + 1 − 2 = 2 → bigger than 0, so the neuron fires: the robot goes to eat! 🔋

CORE AI: "Every thought in this city is really math! Millions of tiny calculations like this one, repeated again and again, let our robots learn, recognize, and create."
Learning = tuning numbers: When an AI "learns", it slowly adjusts its weights until its answers get better - just like practicing until you get it right.
